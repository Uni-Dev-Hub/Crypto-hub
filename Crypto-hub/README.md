# Crypto-hub for KDE Plasma 6

**Crypto-hub** is a modern, feature-rich, Material You styled cryptocurrency market tracker and 100% private crypto portfolio manager designed specifically for **KDE Plasma 6**.

---

## Key Features

- **Built-in Crypto Portfolio (100% Local & Private):**
  - Track real-time portfolio balance, 24h profit/loss (PnL), all-time ROI, and asset allocation breakdown.
  - All data is stored strictly on your device — no accounts, no APIs, zero third-party telemetry.
  - One-click balance privacy mode (hide/show balances).
- **16 Modular Desktop Widget Cards:**
  - Place any of the 16 standalone cards directly on your desktop with responsive auto-scaling.
- **3-Tab Pop-Up Panel Feed:**
  - Fast sliding navigation between **Market Feed**, **Watchlist (Favorites)**, and **Portfolio**.
- **Smart System Notifications (Alerts):**
  - Configurable alert triggers for coins (price targets, 24h breakouts, ATH/ATL), macro metrics (Fear & Greed index, dominance), and Portfolio (balance milestones, 24h surge/drop %, ROI targets).
- **Easy Backup & Restore:**
  - Save all your settings, watchlist, alerts, and portfolio directly to a `.json` backup file or restore anytime.
- **Offline SQLite Coin Search:**
  - Search and add cryptocurrencies instantly offline with a local database cache of thousands of coins.
- **Rate-Limit Protection:**
  - Built-in cascade request scheduler prevents HTTP 429 errors from public market APIs.
- **Dynamic Status Indicator:**
  - Real-time market mood indicator right on your Plasma panel.

## 16 Modular Desktop Widget Cards

When placed directly on the desktop (desktop mode), choose from 16 specialized cards:

1. **Selected Coin Card:** Real-time price, 24h change %, coin logo, active alert count, and portfolio badge.
2. **Crypto Portfolio Card:** Total portfolio balance, 24h gain/loss, and mini asset allocation bar.
3. **Fear & Greed Index:** Market sentiment gauge with visual gradient progress.
4. **Global Market Cap:** Total cryptocurrency market valuation and 24h trend.
5. **24h Trading Volume:** Global 24h market volume tracking.
6. **Dominance Distribution:** Visual breakdown of BTC, ETH, and USDT market share.
7. **Stablecoin Dominance:** Risk-On / Risk-Off market sentiment indicator.
8. **Active Cryptocurrencies:** Total count of active coins on the market.
9. **ETH/BTC Ratio:** Ethereum performance relative to Bitcoin.
10. **BTC Price Valuation (Rainbow Zone):** Logarithmic regression price model (Fire Sale, Buy, Accumulate, Bubble).
11. **BTC Halving Countdown:** Epoch progress bar and days remaining until the next Bitcoin halving.
12. **Marquee Price Ticker:** Smooth live scrolling price ticker for your watchlist.
13. **Top 5 Gainers:** Live leaderboard of top market gainers.
14. **Top 5 Losers:** Live leaderboard of top market losers.
15. **Trending Coins:** Most searched cryptocurrencies right now.
16. **Market Analytics Summary:** Automated condition report of overall market state.
