**Crypto-hub** is a modern, feature-rich, Material You styled cryptocurrency market tracker and 100% private crypto portfolio manager designed specifically for **KDE Plasma 6**. Track real-time prices, global market metrics, portfolio holdings, analytical valuation models, and receive smart desktop notifications.

---

### 16 Modular Desktop Widget Cards
When placed directly on the desktop, choose from 16 standalone card types with responsive scaling:
1. **Selected Coin Card**: Track real-time price, 24h change, coin logo, active alerts count, and portfolio status.
2. **Crypto Portfolio Card**: Dedicated desktop widget showing total portfolio balance, 24h profit/loss, and mini asset allocation bar.
3. **Fear & Greed Index**: Gauge market sentiment with a visual spectrum bar.
4. **Global Market Cap**: Total crypto market valuation and 24h change trend.
5. **24h Trading Volume**: Global 24h market volume tracking.
6. **Dominance Distribution**: Visual breakdown of BTC, ETH, and top coins market share.
7. **Stablecoin Dominance**: Risk-On / Risk-Off market sentiment indicator.
8. **Active Cryptocurrencies**: Total count of active coins on the market.
9. **ETH/BTC Ratio**: Measure Ethereum performance relative to Bitcoin.
10. **BTC Price Valuation (Rainbow Zone)**: Logarithmic regression price model (Fire Sale, Buy, Accumulate, Bubble).
11. **BTC Halving Countdown**: Epoch progress bar and days remaining until the next Bitcoin halving.
12. **Marquee Price Ticker**: Smooth live scrolling price ticker for your watchlist.
13. **Top 5 Gainers**: Live leaderboard of top market gainers.
14. **Top 5 Losers**: Live leaderboard of top market losers.
15. **Trending Coins**: Most searched cryptocurrencies right now.
16. **Market Analytics Summary**: Automated condition report of overall market state.

---

### Key Features
- **Built-in Crypto Portfolio (100% Local & Private)**: Track total balance, 24h profit/loss (PnL in fiat and percentage), all-time ROI based on average buy prices, and asset allocation breakdown. All financial data is stored strictly on your device with zero telemetry and a quick balance privacy mode (hide/show balance).
- **3-Tab Pop-Up Panel**: Smooth sliding navigation between **Market Feed**, custom **Watchlist (Favorites)**, and **Portfolio**.
- **Smart System Notifications (Alerts)**: Set custom triggers for coin prices, 24h high/low breakouts, ATH/ATL reach, Fear & Greed shifts, and portfolio metrics (balance targets, 24h gain/loss %, all-time ROI milestones).
- **Native Backup & Restore**: Export and import complete snapshots of all settings, watchlist, alerts, and portfolio positions directly to a `.json` file with pre-restoration validation.
- **Rate-Limit Safe**: Built-in cascade request scheduler prevents HTTP 429 errors from public market APIs.
- **Offline SQLite Search**: Instant coin search powered by a local SQLite database (works offline).
- **Dynamic Panel Indicator**: Real-time overall market state indicator right on your Plasma panel.
